Where this runs. All encoding and decoding executes in your browser via ONNX Runtime Web in a Web Worker. Your image is never uploaded; no server is involved.

Why your image got downsampled. The decoder allocates ≈ 12 MB of float32 tensor per megapixel plus intermediates. On a 12 MP phone photo this overruns Safari's ~300 MB tab budget. The demo auto-resizes anything above 0.5 MP on mobile or 1 MP on desktop, preserving aspect ratio. The model itself has no such limit — on a phone NPU or server it runs the full image.

Production inference time per pass. Encode and decode are the same INN backbone run forward and reverse, so per-pass cost is approximately equal — one number covers both. Numbers anchor at the model card's target of ≤ 1 s for 12 MP on a phone NPU and scale linearly with pixel count (the INN is fully convolutional). Variance ±2× across hardware generations. The browser-WASM mobile timing you see above is roughly 100–500× slower than the phone NPU production target; server GPU is another ~10× faster on top.
