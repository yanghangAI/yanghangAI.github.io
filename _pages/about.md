## Research

I work on machine learning, invertible neural networks, and robotics. Current interests:

- **Mobile motion capture.** Using a quadruped robot as a mobile platform to record human motion outside the constraints of a fixed studio.
- **Invertible neural networks** for lossless information recovery, applied to deep-learning image steganography ([PRIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13620), [DKiS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18243)).
- **Medical imaging.** Vision transformers for automated disease detection — [CovidViT](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13042-022-01676-7) reaches 98% on chest X-rays.
- **Robotics.** Legged-robot perception, autonomy, and human–robot interaction.
